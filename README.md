# ThermoGap

**Industrial waste-heat reuse to make additional products at the same factory.**

[Español](README.es.md)

ThermoGap is a research-stage concept for evaluating whether heat rejected by one process can support another product or process at the same industrial site. It considers physical compatibility, schedules, equipment, energy use, costs, and uncertainty.

## Current status

**Research further. No production software has been built.** The research found that heat-source matching, process integration, and time-dependent storage are already addressed by existing tools. A useful product would need to prove a specific gap, such as linking recovered heat to a new product, its feedstock, and its incremental economics. This is a hypothesis, not a proven advantage.

## What the research covers

- Potential sources and uses of industrial waste heat.
- New-build and retrofit requirements.
- Commercial product routes within the same factory.
- Technology, timing, temperature, and economic constraints.
- Three synthetic cases and a critical review.
- Evidence and limitations for the United States, Europe, and Canada.

## Evidence labels

The reports separate verified facts, engineering assumptions, model predictions, commercial hypotheses, and unresolved questions. Case inputs are synthetic. They are not data from operating factories. No experiment or industrial deployment was performed.

## Limitations

The work is a literature and product review with hand-checkable synthetic calculations. It is not an independently benchmarked software model, a plant audit, an equipment design, or proof of profitability. Project-specific prices, operating data, buyer demand, and installation quotes are still required.

## Decision

**RESEARCH FURTHER.** Compare the same transparent cases in established tools before building an MVP.
